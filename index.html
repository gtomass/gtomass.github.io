About

I am an astrophysicist studying binaries in their late stages of stellar evolution, with a focus on asymptotic giant branch (AGB) and post-AGB stars and their dusty circumstellar environments. I combine high-angular-resolution observations, radiative transfer modelling and 3D radiation-hydrodynamics simulations to understand how evolved stars lose mass, form dust, and how companions shape their atmospheres and outflows.

I am currently pursuing my PhD at Observvatoire de la Côte d'Azur in Nice, France under the supervision of Eric Lagadec and Andrea Chiavassa.

Research

Modelling

Dusty circumstellar environments and SED modelling

The spectral energy distributions of dusty evolved stars carry the imprint of their mass-loss history. I developed a fitting code coupling SED fitting with 1D radiative transfer to constrain the dust distribution and properties around these stars.

Observations

High-angular-resolution imaging with SPHERE/VLT

Using SPHERE on ESO's Very Large Telescope, I resolved the circumstellar environment of AFGL 4106 in unprecedented detail, leading to a reclassification of the system [from … to …]. The images were featured as ESO Picture of the Week.

Simulations

3D radiation-hydrodynamics of AGB stars with companions

With the 3D radiation-hydrodynamics code CO5BOLD, I study how a close companion affects the convective atmosphere of an AGB star. I extended the code to include a companion in the gravitational potential, and restored and optimised its vectorisation, achieving a fivefold speed-up that benefits all CO5BOLD users.

Software

DustyPY

A public tool to fit spectral energy distributions of dusty stars with 1D radiative transfer models and MCMC fitting methods. This tool is develloped in Python and is available on GitHub.
